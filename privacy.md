App Name: UpperDot (com.mail2dev.upperdot).

Local-First Storage: Data is stored locally using Room DB.

Google Drive Usage: Google Drive is accessed only via [https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata](https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata) to store private backups, receipt photos, and voice notes in the user's hidden app data directory.

Third-Party Data Sharing: Zero user data is transmitted to third-party servers, tracking networks, or custom backends.
